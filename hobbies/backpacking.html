Backpacking

A refreshing reset built around forests, mountains, long walks, and the very simple goal of keeping moving.

adventurehikingcamping

How I got into it

I was very skeptical of camping and backpacking for many years until a close friend convinced me to try backpacking for the first time. Before long, I began to enjoy backpacking, camping, and hiking on a regular basis. The inability to work on anything and the new goalpost—explore as much as possible and keep moving—make backpacking a particularly refreshing reset and productivity booster for me.

What I do

I train for difficult hikes and visit forests and mountains across the country. I plan group trips with my friends and go out as often as I can. I am also planning on starting an NYU Nature Club.
