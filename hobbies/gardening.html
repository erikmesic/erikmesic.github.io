GARDENING · DEC 2007 – PRESENT

Gardening

A lifelong love of plants, quiet outdoor spaces, and the slightly ridiculous pleasure of turning a patch of ground into something new.

plantsnaturePacific Northwest

Where it started

I practically grew up gardening with my mom. By the time I was a toddler, I had memorized most of the names of native plants in the Pacific Northwest and hundreds of plants that we commonly interacted with.

Why I love it

I love the peace and quiet of the outdoors and the ability to terraform spaces to fit certain aesthetics and goals. I also love and appreciate plants for their natural beauty, and I enjoy simply being around them.

What I do

I have not been able to garden as much as I would like recently, but I help my mom set up her garden whenever I can.

Little victories

Terraforming my own section of a yard and becoming something of an expert in Pacific Northwest flora.

A very old hobby

Since December 2007. Long before spreadsheets, debate rounds, or anything resembling a résumé.
