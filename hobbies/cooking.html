COOKING · AUG 2024 – PRESENT

Cooking

What started as necessity turned into a way to appreciate culture, learn something new, and feed people I like.

foodculturelearning

How it started

I only started cooking out of necessity, since I became a vegetarian and my family insisted that I cook my own food. Since then, though, I have learned to use food to appreciate my culture and grow closer to others.

What I like to cook

I have especially enjoyed cooking South Asian, European, and African dishes. I am also very interested in learning Latin American, East Asian, and Southeast Asian recipes.

What I do

I search for interesting recipes when I have time. I cook for myself, friends, and parents when possible, and I learn new techniques to cook food more effectively.

Things I'm proud of

  • Reconnecting my dad with his culture through food
  • Cooking fufu from scratch
  • Cooking bitter melon
  • Mastering paneer butter masala

Current menu

Still learning. Still looking for recipes. Still very willing to cook for friends.
