PHILOSOPHY · JAN 2024 – PRESENT

Philosophy

The hobby of discovering that almost every ordinary question is secretly a philosophical question.

ideasepistemologyontologydebate

Why I got hooked

Until I took my first philosophy class, I did not realize that philosophy is everywhere. In my current opinion, even the most basic everyday questions that we ask ourselves boil down to a matter of philosophy. I love philosophy because it informs the way we think even if we do not think about it that way; it is the basis of our values and our reasoning, and understanding our philosophical beliefs is key to better understanding ourselves.

What I do

I write about philosophical ideas and take time to think through concepts in my free time. I talk through difficult and controversial ideas with my friends to better understand different perspectives. I engage with debate and read philosophical arguments ranging from epistemology to ontology.

People I keep arguing with

I am quite critical of existentialist thinkers, even if I share many of their beliefs, and I have recently become fascinated with Hobbes. I tend to agree with Descartes about “I think therefore I am,” but I am critical of many of his beliefs. I find Rousseau's conception of the social contract convincing, but believe there are numerous caveats. I think Rawls got many ideas correct, while I am more critical of contemporary thinkers like Nussbaum. I am interested in learning more about Nietzsche and Simone de Beauvoir.

Where I land

I consider myself largely stoic and influenced by Christianity.

A useful pastime

Convincing my friends to think about life in certain ways is, apparently, an accomplishment.
