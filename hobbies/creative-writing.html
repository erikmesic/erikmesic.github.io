Why I write

I have always liked writing, and I have wanted to write a book for many years, but most projects fizzled out as whoever I wanted to work with slowly lost motivation. I appreciate the way writing gives us a permanent way to put our experiences onto paper, and I admire the ways poems and prose use wordplay to bring meaning into written language.

What I think writing does

I think writing is arguably the tool that brought humans from the stone age to where we are today. Working with persuasion, emotion, and ideas through writing is endlessly fascinating to me.

What I do

I write short stories about important experiences in my life when I have time. I write poems or prose whenever I feel a flash of inspiration.

Things I've managed to finish

  • 1st Place, Spring H.C.T.E. Writing Awards, Senior Nonfiction
  • Reached the 100-page mark on my autobiographical anthology
  • Convinced a friend to start their own autobiographical anthology
  • Helped friends become more open with their thoughts and feelings

Current problem

Having more ideas than time to write them down.
