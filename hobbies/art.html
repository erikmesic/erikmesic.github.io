Art

Making things, collecting things, and trying to understand why a strange painting can stay in your head for years.

paintingcollectingexpressionism

Why it matters

Beyond the pompousness of it all, creating and collecting art is particularly meaningful to me. I will never forget a painting I purchased that depicts an eccentric-looking cat. On the surface, it appears a bit off-putting; however, on the back, a newspaper clipping describes how the artist suffered a stroke and could no longer speak. She found her voice through art, which I find to be reflective of art's value more generally: expressing that which is hard to put directly into words.

What I make

I create art whenever I feel inspiration, whether that is on a plane or in-between studying. I have made more than 50 unique artworks, including a 24-square-foot painting of a Qing Dynasty emperor.

What I collect

I purchase art from antique shops whenever I can, looking for features like composition, artist, and date created.

Favorites

I particularly enjoy expressionist and impressionist art. I am a huge fan of oil paintings, even though I have yet to experiment with them myself. I enjoy abstract art more than realistic art, although I do not particularly like nonobjective art.

On the wall

AP 2-D Art and Design, 50+ artworks, and an ever-growing list of paintings I would like to own.
