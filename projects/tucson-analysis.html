Assessing Tucson Property: A Deep Analysis

40-page analysis of single-family rental options and vacancy scenarios, combining financial modeling, tax considerations, and market comparisons.

Overview

Researched a single-family residential rental property in Tucson, AZ. Produced a detailed 40-page report evaluating options after a distressed scenario, and delivered eight accepted recommendations to the owner.

Analysis

Covered rental market dynamics, regulatory considerations, expected in-period expenses, and tenant screening. Compared alternative investment and financing scenarios.

Modeling

Built a Python MCMC model to project returns and simulate short-term rental scenarios. Compared opportunity costs with ETFs, HYSAs, and like-kind property investments in multiple cities.

Recommendations

  1. Short-term rental pivot assessment with marketing and security measures.
  2. Tax-optimization through like-kind exchange considerations.
  3. Operational suggestions for property management and tenant screening.
