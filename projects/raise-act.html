RAISE Act Legislative Advocacy

Targeted legislative advocacy to improve conditions and funding for rural and underrepresented students in Florida.

Overview

Spearheaded targeted changes to improve conditions for rural, impoverished, and underrepresented students, creating a $19,400 bonus for Florida accelerated course teachers on average. Modeled redirected educational supplement funding to maintain cost-neutrality and partnered with stakeholders to build bipartisan support.

Role

  • Led policy modeling and financial projections totaling ~$600 million in redirected funding.
  • Organized outreach to 50+ legislators and stakeholder organizations to gather support.
  • Coordinated with educational organizations and secured meetings with key executives.

Methods

Used spreadsheet modeling, stakeholder mapping, and direct outreach. Integrated fiscal analysis with policy framing to present a cost-neutral implementation plan.

Results

Achieved bipartisan support, drafted recommended statutory language, and secured endorsements from educational organizations and local leaders. The plan included durable funding shifts and policy safeguards.
